FAQs

Frequently Asked Questions

The questions cottagers and homeowners across Lambton County ask us most.

How fast can you get to a cottage in Grand Bend when the heat quits?

We are based right in Grand Bend, so a local technician is usually close by. Propane furnace failures on a January weekend get priority, and we will give you an honest arrival window when you call (548) 554-3151.

Can you service a propane furnace in a seasonal cottage?

Yes. Most lakeside homes around Grand Bend sit off the gas main and run propane, and we work on those systems all the time. We coordinate with your fuel supplier and make sure the unit is safe to run after sitting idle for weeks.

Is a ductless mini-split a good fit for a Lake Huron cottage?

For seasonal cottages and lakeside additions with no ductwork, mini-splits are usually the right call. One quiet unit heats through lake-wind cold snaps and dehumidifies on humid beach days, sized so it does not short-cycle.

Can you convert our cottage from propane to a heat pump?

Yes. We remove the old propane furnace, install a cold-climate heat pump sized to carry the full heating load through a Lambton County winter, and handle the rebate paperwork so the conversion pencils out.

Do you set up smart thermostats so the cottage is warm before we arrive?

All the time. We install and program smart thermostats that warm the cottage before you drive up and protect your pipes during the empty stretches in shoulder season, when a sudden freeze can do real damage.

Which lakeside communities around Grand Bend do you cover?

We cover Grand Bend plus Port Franks, Thedford, Parkhill, and Forest, along with the cottages and year-round homes dotted along the Lake Huron shoreline in between.

Can you add backup power for winter outages?

Yes. As a Generac dealer we sell, install, and service standby generators that keep your furnace, well pump, and lights on through lake-effect storms and rural outages, so a lakeside cottage or year-round home never goes cold and dark when the grid drops.
